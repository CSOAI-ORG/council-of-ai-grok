## Scope
Signed-measurement artifacts, signature/Merkle verification paths, and provenance between this repository and the live board (https://councilof.ai/api/gspc). Methodology discussion goes to the corrections ledger (https://councilof.ai/corrections/).

## Commitments
Acknowledgement within 5 business days; fixes for accepted reports within 90 days; disclosure only after a fix ships; corrections are ledger entries, not silent edits. Measurement, never certification.
